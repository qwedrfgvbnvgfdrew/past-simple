How the Past Simple works

When do we use it? Qachon ishlatamiz?

Finished actions
I drank tea this morning.Men bugun ertalab choy ichdim.
Past habits
We played outside every day.Biz har kuni tashqarida o‘ynardik.
Stories in order
He opened the door and left.U eshikni ochdi va ketdi.

Positive sentences (+) Darak gaplar

SubjectVerb + edExample
I · You · He · She · It · We · TheyworkedShe worked in a bank.U bankda ishlagan edi.
Good news: every subject uses the same form. No -s for he, she, it!Hamma shaxslar uchun bir xil shakl. he, she, it uchun -s yo‘q!

Spelling rules for regular verbs Imlo qoidalari

Most verbs + ed
work → worked
play → played
Ends in e + d
live → lived
like → liked
Consonant + y y → ied
study → studied
cry → cried
Short verb, 1 vowel + 1 consonant double it
stop → stopped
plan → planned

Negative sentences (−) Inkor gaplar

subject + didn’t + base verb

I didn’t like the film.Men filmni yoqtirmadim. She didn’t eat breakfast.U nonushta qilmadi.
She didn’t went home. ✗
She didn’t go home. ✓
After didn’t, the verb goes back to its base form.didn’t dan keyin fe‘l asosiy shaklda turadi.

Questions (?) So‘roq gaplar

Did + subject + base verb ?

Did you play tennis?Siz tennis o‘ynadingizmi?Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.

Where did he live?U qayerda yashagan? What time did you get up?Siz soat nechada turdingiz?
Did already shows the past, so the verb stays base: Did she play, not Did she played.
